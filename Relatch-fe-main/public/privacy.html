01Overview

Relatch turns a document into a skill file that Claude or Codex can follow. This policy says what we collect, why, and who else touches it. We are a small team and we keep it plain.

It applies to relatch.online and app.relatch.online. By using the app you agree to it. If you do not, please do not use Relatch.

02What we collect

Account

You can try Relatch once without an account. After that you sign in with Google or GitHub through Clerk. Relatch receives a basic profile from that provider: your name, email address and provider user ID. We never receive your Google or GitHub password. We use this to sign you in, scope your skills to you, and reply to you.

Your documents

Digital PDFs, Word files, text, Markdown, code and data files are read in your browser. The text is then sent to our server so a model can write your skill. Scanned or image-based files are sent to an OCR provider first. We do not keep the documents you upload, and we do not keep their text. They exist only for as long as it takes to produce your skill.

Finished skills

A finished skill is the file Relatch writes. In your browser, recent skills are kept in local storage so you can reopen and download them. Where skill history is switched on for your account, finished skills are also saved to our database so they follow you across devices. You can pause or clear history in settings. Only the finished skill is ever saved. Never your original document, its file name or its text.

Usage and limits

To enforce the daily and weekly limits we keep counters tied to your account ID in Upstash Redis. They expire on their own. Visitors without an account get a short-lived token stored in the browser, and we use the IP address of the request to limit abuse of the free try.

Logs

Our servers log events such as errors and limits being reached, in Axiom. A log entry records which part of Relatch was called, the outcome, the reason, your account ID if you are signed in, and your IP address. Some entries can include a file name, for example when a file is too large. Logs are meant to record outcomes, not the content of your documents.

Messages you send us

If you use the contact form or join a waitlist, we receive the name, email address and message you type. We use it only to reply to you about Relatch.

03Analytics and session replay

We use these tools to understand how people use the product:

  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager collect page views, session length, device type and a general region.
  • PostHog records product events and session replays. A replay is a recording of what happens on your screen in the app, such as clicks, scrolling and the text you type into fields, other than passwords. Because a replay shows the screen, it can also show what is displayed there, including a finished skill.

You can block these tools with your browser settings or an extension. You can also use Google's opt-out add-on for Google Analytics. We have not yet added an in-app control to turn session replay off.

04Who else processes your data

Relatch uses other companies to run. Each handles your data under its own terms. We send them only what the job needs.

  • Clerk: sign-in and sessions. Google and GitHub: sign-in providers.
  • LlamaParse (LlamaIndex), with Datalab as a fallback: OCR for scanned files.
  • TypeSafe Jev, through OpenRouter: reads excerpts of your document, together with its file name, to work out what kind of document it is and which parts matter. It returns labels, not text.
  • Google Gemini API: writes your skill for most documents.
  • OpenRouter, routed to Groq, as a fallback if the first model fails. For long or complex documents, or when a draft fails our quality check, open-weight models from DeepSeek and Z.ai (GLM) through OpenRouter, on hosts that OpenRouter lists as zero data retention.
  • Upstash (usage counters), Axiom (logs), Neon (saved skill history), Vercel (hosting).
  • Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, PostHog: analytics.
  • SplitForms: contact and waitlist messages.

If every model is unavailable, Relatch builds a basic skill from templates on our own servers, with no outside AI call. Before any of this starts, a check on your device and our server screens out corrupted files and unsupported formats. Files that fail are dropped and not sent to any provider.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your documents to train models. We cannot control what a provider does on its own systems, so the provider's policy applies there. We encourage you to read them.

05Cookies and browser storage

Clerk uses cookies and session storage to keep you signed in. Relatch uses local storage for your skills, your agent choice, your background motion setting, and the free-try token. Google Analytics and PostHog set analytics cookies. Sign-in storage is needed for the app to work. Analytics cookies are not, and you can block them.

06How long we keep things

  • Documents and their text: not kept after your skill is made.
  • Finished skills: until you delete them or your account, when history is on. In your browser, until you clear them or clear your browser data.
  • Account details: while your account is active.
  • Usage counters: they expire within days.
  • Logs and analytics: held by Axiom, Google and PostHog under their own retention settings.
  • Contact and waitlist messages: held by SplitForms for up to 30 days, or until you ask us to remove them.

07Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account, your saved skills, or any message you sent us. You can ask what we hold about you. Email hello@relatch.online from the address on your account and we will act on reasonable requests within a reasonable time. You can also pause or clear skill history in settings, and clear Relatch's data from your browser at any time.

08Children

Relatch is not for children and we do not knowingly collect their information. If a minor has sent us their details, tell us and we will remove them.
